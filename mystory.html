I failed my first computer science class in high school. I wasn’t sure if I just didn’t get it—or if I wasn’t meant for tech at all. But something about the challenge stuck with me. I couldn’t let go of the feeling that if I just looked at it from the right angle, I could figure it out. Fast forward a few years: I’m now earning my Bachelor’s in Computer Science and my MBA in Financial Engineering from the University of Alabama, with multiple software engineering internships under my belt. What changed? Not just my skills—but my mindset. I learned how to break things down, how to persist through complexity, and how to build systems that work in the real world. That first failure didn’t stop me—it redirected me. In May of 2025, I graduated from The University of Alabama Magna Cum Laude with a degree in Computer Science. Upon graduating, I traveled to Huntsville, Alabama for a software engineering internship with Lockheed Martin Space. Fall of 2025 I will be making a return to Tuscaloosa to finish my MBA at the Culverhouse College of Business with a concentration in Financial Engineering.
Overview:

I worked on this project in Lisbon, Portugal with a sustainable wind energy company Windcredible. This is a cost estimation web tool for wind turbine installations using Blazor and ASP.NET Core, enabling clients to calculate potential savings based on location specific inputs. Deployed the project internally on a zero cost in house IIS server. Integrated global GIS datasets into PostgreSQL database with PostGIS, using pgAdmin for database management and location based queries.
